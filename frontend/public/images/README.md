These photographs provide general sports context. They do not depict venues contained in the historical dataset.

- `multi-sport-venue.webp` — original AI-generated contextual photograph for the Activities & services page.
- `urban-sports-complex.webp` — original AI-generated contextual photograph for the Source regions page.
- `community-venue-review.webp` — original AI-generated contextual photograph shared by the Add a venue and Community venues pages.

The three contextual photographs are explicitly labelled in the interface as illustrative and do not represent listed or verified venues. Each is resized to 1,200 × 500 pixels and compressed as WebP; the largest is under 150 KB, and no page loads more than one of them.

The four SVG visual guides (`activities.svg`, `source-regions.svg`, `data-lineage.svg` and `community-review.svg`) are original project illustrations. The data-lineage diagram remains active where a diagram communicates the process better; the others are retained as lightweight project assets.

The original JPEG source files are retained outside the deployable `public` directory in `docs/image-sources/`. The two WebP files loaded by the overview are resized and compressed to reduce their combined transfer size by about 60%.
